Global Hide and Show Chatter

In Odoo, the "Hide and Show Chatter" feature refers to toggling the visibility of the Chatter widget in a form view. Chatter in Odoo is a communication tool within the form view of records that allows users to interact through messages, notes, and activity logs on individual records. It is often used to track changes, add comments, or log activities for specific records, such as sales orders, invoices, or leads.

Hide and Show Chat

In Odoo, "Hide Chat" refers to a feature that allows users to minimize or hide the chat panel in the Odoo interface. The chat panel in Odoo is typically located on the right side of the screen, where users can engage in direct messages, group chats, and discussions across the platform. The "Hide Chat" functionality helps improve the user experience by giving users the option to focus on other tasks without the distraction of incoming messages or a visible chat panel.

Hide and Show Chatter by Groups

In Odoo,"Show Chat" is a feature that allows users to display or expand the chat panel, which is typically located on the right side of the Odoo interface. This panel provides access to Odoo's internal messaging system, allowing users to communicate in real-time with colleagues through direct messages, group chats, and discussion channels.
